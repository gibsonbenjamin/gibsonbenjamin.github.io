SQL · Tableau · SQLite · R
S&P 500 Risk & Return Dashboard
CSV Data → SQLite → SQL / R → Tableau
Analyzed 10 years of S&P 500 price data for 500+ stocks. Routed data through a SQLite database, calculated annualized returns, volatility, and Sharpe ratios via SQL window functions, and visualized results by sector in Tableau.
Key Findings
  • Information Technology led risk-adjusted returns (median Sharpe 0.64); Consumer Staples (0.33) and Real Estate (0.35) lagged.
  • Higher risk did not guarantee higher reward: Energy had the highest volatility (~40%) but a below-average Sharpe (0.37).

Electronics Sales Dashboard
CSV Data → Tableau
Analyzed nearly 49,000 global electronics sales transactions across 8 product categories and multiple continents. Built 5 visualizations covering sales trends, profit analysis, geographic distribution, and channel breakdown.
Key Findings
  • The U.S. drove 53% of ~$43.6M in sales (UK 13%, Canada 8%).
  • In-person purchases were about 69% of sales vs. 31% online.
  • Strong seasonality: sales peak every December and drop sharply each April.

R · Statistical Modeling
Customer Churn Analysis
CSV Data → R → Classification Tree
Analyzed a dataset of 7,000 telecom customers to predict churn using a decision tree model in R. Performed binning and cross-table analysis across 11 predictors, achieving 79.5% validation accuracy with contract type, tenure, and payment method as the strongest predictors.
Key Findings
  • 26.7% overall churn; month-to-month contracts churned at 42.9% vs. 2.9% for two-year contracts.
  • First-year customers churned at 47.6%; electronic check payers at 45.4%.
  • Recommended annual-contract discounts, priority support for new customers, and incentives to move off electronic check.

Nationals Player Stats Analysis
Raw Data → Excel → Access DB → Insights
Analyzed 2019 season batting and pitching stats for 50 Washington Nationals players. Used Excel for VLOOKUP, conditional formatting, and charting, and built a relational Access database with queries, forms, and reports to identify top performers.
Key Findings
